UPTET Saathi Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

UPTET Saathi is a free study app for UPTET, Super TET and CTET candidates. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

What the app stores

The app saves the following on your phone only: your chosen language and exam, your answers, scores and test settings, the questions you are revising, and how much you studied each day. This information is never sent to us or to anyone else. There is no account or sign-up, and UPTET Saathi has no server.

What the app collects or shares

Nothing. The app does not collect personal information, does not use analytics or advertising, and does not share any data with third parties. It does not ask for your location, contacts, camera or microphone.

Links that leave the app

Some buttons open other apps or websites: sending feedback opens a Google Form in your browser, the home banner opens our WhatsApp group, and sharing the app opens your phone's share menu. Anything you type or send there is handled by Google, WhatsApp or the app you choose, under their own privacy policies. If you send feedback, we receive only what you write in the form and the ID of a question you report.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in your phone's settings, deletes everything it has saved. We cannot recover it, because we never had a copy.

Children

The app is meant for adults preparing for teacher eligibility exams and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
